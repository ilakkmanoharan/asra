From Databricks Meets PyTorch
to Databricks Meets Keras

Two architectural patterns for enterprise AI — research flexibility vs operational simplicity — and how ASRA implements both on the path to scientific intelligence.

Databricks + PyTorch = Infrastructure + Research Flexibility
Databricks + Keras = Infrastructure + Simplicity

Databricks meets PyTorch

Enterprise data/compute + maximum model development control.

  • Lakehouse, Spark pipelines, features, MLOps
  • Custom architectures, RL, world models, LLMs
  • Frontier research — build anything
"Unify data and training in one environment."

Databricks meets Keras

Same infrastructure + radical ease of use.

  • Abstracted pipelines, tracking, deployment
  • model.fit(data) — complexity hidden
  • Domain experts focus on problems, not clusters
"A full AI factory that feels like a workbench."

Unified platform flow (ASRA example)

Environment→ State transitions→ Lakehouse storage→ Distributed processing→ PyTorch training→ World model updates→ Eval & deploy
